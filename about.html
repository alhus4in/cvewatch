The problem

New software vulnerabilities are published every day. Most students and small teams do not have the time to read long security reports, so important patches get missed.

Our solution

CVEWatch lists each vulnerability in one short row: what it is, what it affects and how serious it is. Users can sign up to get alerts about Critical CVEs.
