Security tip

Severity guide

Every CVE gets a CVSS score from 0 to 10. The higher the score, the faster you should patch.

  • Critical (9.0 to 10.0): patch immediately
  • High (7.0 to 8.9): patch within a few days
  • Medium (4.0 to 6.9): patch within a week or two
  • Low (0.1 to 3.9): patch during normal maintenance

How to stay safe

  1. Turn on automatic updates for your system and apps.
  2. Patch Critical and High CVEs first.
  3. Remove software you no longer use.
  4. Check the vendor's security page for fixes.

Tip: a CVE ID looks like CVE-YEAR-NUMBER.
